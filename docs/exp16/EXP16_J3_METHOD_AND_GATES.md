# Exp16 J3 region CE method and execution boundaries

The user authorized three new J3 arms, seed42 → seed142 → seed242, and six frozen VAL passes only after all training and independent audits pass. The base is Exp15 J-E2 commit `401210427a61f3430f18bb4fe93261c911f66080`. Existing J0/J1 models and their VAL receipts are immutable comparison inputs. No TEST, extra seeds, J2, image generation or thesis work is authorized.

## Method

J3 uses the native YOLO11n-seg fused P3 layer16, 64 channels at stride8. The unmodified Exp15 region extractor binds augmented native overlap-mask IDs to their area-ordered Polygon classes. Each P3 cell is weighted by its foreground fraction in the corresponding native stride4 mask. The existing MLP, `Linear(64,128) → ReLU → Linear(128,128)`, produces an L2-normalized 128-dimensional region representation. A new `Linear(128,7)` classifier predicts the original seven defect classes.

CE supervises all valid pooled regions with ordinary independent seven-class softmax cross-entropy. Each represented class has equal total loss weight, with equal weight among its valid regions. There is no label smoothing, dataset class weight or additional sampler.

The first engineering prototype restricted CE to J1's eligible anchors. Independent pre-freeze review identified that J1's non-anchor embeddings can still receive candidate gradients; an anchor-only CE would withhold some singleton and rare-class labels. The final design therefore uses the same full valid pooled-region availability as J1. The losses still have different anchor qualifications, class-weight normalization and gradient support, inherent to their definitions. The initial prototype and all zero-update diagnostic evidence are retained separately. Source-group duplicates remain ordinary native instances; CE has no Pair exclusion or extra sampling. Small or fully occluded polygons without native raster pixels remain excluded from both auxiliary methods. Main-task samples are never changed to add anchors.

J3's six head parameter tensors include the same four MLP tensors as J1 and two classifier tensors. Head initialization is scoped inside a CPU RNG fork. There is no auxiliary BN or dropout. Main and auxiliary losses share one native forward and one AdamW update. The classifier introduces a necessary head difference; a CE–SupCon result alone does not causally isolate every difference in head geometry and optimization.

## TRAIN-only strength calibration

CE weight is not copied from J1. Before formal training, eight fixed real TRAIN batches `(1,7,11,21,32,42,56,70)` are inspected at COCO initialization and the two already frozen J-A step70 backbone states. The CE head starts from the same deterministic fresh state at each backbone snapshot. Independent native and auxiliary gradients measure per-layer norms, conflict and global clipping effects. The feasible fixed-weight interval requires every snapshot-median weighted Backbone ratio to lie between 1% and 10%, and every probe ratio to remain at most19%. The preferred value targets the largest snapshot median at5%, clamped to that interval. Two-significant-digit rounding stays within the feasible interval; an empty interval stops preparation. The actual native–joint cosine must be at least0.98. This is an analytical coefficient choice from TRAIN gradients, without a grid of optimizer runs or VAL selection.

The first prototype's unscaled CE derivative was inaccurate under the native AMP graph. Independent zero-update tests found seven tensors quantized to zero in one snapshot, all connected again after numerical scaling. Scales16,384 and65,536 converged with finite gradients and all120 Backbone tensors connected. Calibration therefore measures `grad(16384 * CE) / 16384` in FP32. This conditions only the independent gradient measurement; formal AMP, GradScaler and the joint loss are unchanged. After selecting the coefficient, the actual native-AMP joint derivative is recomputed on fresh copies of all24 fixed probes, including finite values, norms, cosine and clipping. The original failures, scripts and restored-state proofs remain separate evidence.

These early snapshots do not predict later strength after the CE classifier learns. The final fixed coefficient, measurement details, actual joint-gradient checks and all failed preparation evidence belong in the frozen machine protocol and calibration receipts. No VAL outcome is used for selection.

## Formal fairness and safety

The frozen Exp15 data and main-task protocol are retained: 668 TRAIN images, 1,266 original instances, 640 input, batch32, AdamW, 100 epochs, 2,100 effective updates, 63 warmup positions, native augmentation and sampling, FP32 master parameters, CUDA AMP and global L2 clipping at10. Each arm independently starts from its corresponding seed's frozen COCO transfer. Every step must match the historical J0/J1 tensor hash and learning rate, with the same J1 Polygon geometry and valid pooling set. CE supervision counts are recorded separately from J1 active-anchor counts. The independent audit compares all 2,100 raw-source, augmentation, instance-exposure, optimizer and numeric records against both historical arms.

Each seed has an independent Smoke budget of at most five attempts, with one planned update. Formal training uses separate fresh outputs and budgets. Checkpoints are retained at step1 and every five epochs, with the unique evaluation input being epoch100 `last.pt`'s FP32 main model. Strict reload checks the full head, EMA, AdamW and GradScaler, followed by head removal and native loading. At each formal start, remaining expected checkpoint storage plus 2 GiB must be available. Historical data, environments, weights and failed outputs are retained.

Failure of data binding, auxiliary connectivity, updates, finite numerics, AMP execution, checkpoint reload, matched native trajectory or resources stops progression. No automatic retries, seed changes, coefficient changes, checkpoint substitution or historical cleanup are allowed.
