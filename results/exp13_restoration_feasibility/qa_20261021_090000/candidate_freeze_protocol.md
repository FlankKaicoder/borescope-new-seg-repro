# Candidate freeze protocol (not executed)

No candidate or source is frozen by this archive. Freeze requires a separately authorized, traceable human review and a versioned decision record.

For each proposed candidate, reviewers must establish all of the following from the aligned evidence: (1) the visible original defect is actually removed, not merely retained at low strength; (2) no new abnormal hole, fastener, seam, component or other structure appears; (3) original physical geometry and boundaries are preserved; (4) repaired metal texture and illumination are plausible, not merely smooth; (5) changes outside the edit region are acceptable and explicitly documented; (6) source, masks, output, transform and crop extent correspond, with cropped-away defect area excluded from any repair claim.

Reviewers must record candidate-level evidence and disagreements; source-level labels and image metrics cannot substitute. Variants from one source remain grouped and count as at most one independent source in any feasibility summary. A frozen set must report unique sources separately from candidate variants and state its evidence base. If polygon annotations guide masks, sampling, eligibility or representation losses, explicitly disclose annotation-derived supervision; do not claim pure self-supervision without qualification.

This protocol defines prerequisites only. It does not freeze any pair, pass a Restoration Quality Gate, authorize model selection, training, generation or Exp13.2-B.
